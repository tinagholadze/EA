Who we are

A non-profit that turns ideas into businesses

Entrepreneurs Association is a non-for-profit, membership-based organization founded in 2012. Our work starts from a simple belief: innovation and entrepreneurship should be open to young people everywhere, not only in the capital.

Vision

A culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among young people, with a focus on empowering founders in rural areas of Georgia.

Mission

Build a community of innovators and entrepreneurs, and support them through networking, mentoring, training and acceleration.
